Commune

Tokens and handoff

Every colour in the redesign, mapped to the variable it replaces in app/globals.css. Four are decisions rather than substitutions — those come first.

01

Four decisions

These change how the app looks, not just what the variables are called. Everything after this section is a straight substitution.

1 Mentions are orange, not blue

Was--mention #e4eefc · --mention-foreground #1d4f93 Now--accent-surface #fef3ec · --accent-fg #8a3308

The blue mention chip was the last borrowed colour in the app. A mention is the most important highlight in a chat client, so it uses the brand accent. This retires --mention, --mention-foreground and --mention-self.

2 The sidebar split into a rail and a column

WasOne 260px --sidebar, dark in both themes Now64px --rail (near-black, both themes) + 272px --bg-col (themed)

This is the change that stops it reading as a Slack clone. The rail keeps the dark anchor; the column joins the theme, so light mode is genuinely light.

3 Warm greys became neutral

Was#f3f3f0 · #e4e4df · #1b1b1a · #6e6e69 Now#f5f5f5 · #e9e9e9 · #171717 · #6b6b6b

You asked for grey-black over grey-orange. Neutral also stops the greys competing with #F05710, which is warm and needs a cool ground to stay loud.

4 Destructive got darker

Was#c93b3b — 3.9:1 on white Now#b42318 — 5.0:1 on white

The old red only worked as a button fill. The redesign uses red as text — "Couldn’t send", "Delete message" — which needs 4.5:1.

06

Still open

What the redesign does not yet cover, in the order I would close it.

Gap Detail
Reminders Your reminders-list.tsx sits on the Activity page. The feed and its empty state are designed; reminders are not.
Bookmark editing bookmarks-bar.tsx supports add, rename and delete. The bar is designed; the editing is not.
Huddle in a DM All nine huddle states assume a channel.
Full emoji picker The composer shows frequently-used. The real one is emoji-mart with search and categories — its theme variables are already wired in globals.css.
Someone’s full profile The hover card exists, but "View profile" has nowhere to go.
Token duplication These tokens are copy-pasted into sixteen files. Moving them to globals.css is the first implementation task, not a later cleanup.
Palette versions The command palette exists in three files at different widths. Build from the one in Search and Notification States.
