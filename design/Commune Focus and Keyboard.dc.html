The ring

Two rules. Anything you click gets a hard 2px accent outline at 2px offset, keyboard-only. Anything you type into gets a soft 3px accent halo plus an accent border, on click and keyboard alike — because a text field needs to look active while you use it, not just while you tab to it.

01

Tab order

Seven stops from the top of the page to the send button. Lists collapse to one stop each, so Tab crosses the app in seconds and the arrows do the work inside.

0 Skip to conversation Hidden until focused, then it appears over the rail. Without it, reaching a message means tabbing past every channel.
1 Workspace and rail One stop for the workspace button, then one for the rail as a group — up and down move between Channels, Activity, DMs, Saved.
2 Search Also reachable from anywhere with ⌘K, which is the path most people will use.
3 Channel list One stop. Up and down move through channels and DMs; Enter opens; the list remembers where you were.
4 Channel header Channel name, then the header actions — members, pins, Huddle.
5 Message list One stop. Up and down move between messages, left and right along a message’s actions.
6 Composer Formatting controls sit after the field in the DOM, so Tab from the field reaches them rather than leaving the composer.
7 Send The last stop. Enter sends from the field itself, so most people never land here.

02

Keys

What each key does once focus is inside the app. Nothing here overrides a browser default.

Tab Move to the next region. Never traps, except inside an open modal.
↑ ↓ Move within the focused list — channels, messages, palette results, member lists.
← → Move along a focused message’s action bar, or between tabs in a tablist.
Enter Open the focused thing. In the composer, send.
Esc Close the topmost thing: palette, popover, modal, thread panel — one layer per press, and focus returns to whatever opened it.
⌘K Search, from anywhere including inside a modal.

03

Modals and popovers

Three obligations, all of which are currently missing from the app.

1 Focus moves in On open, focus goes to the first field — or the dialog itself when there is nothing to type into. Never left behind on the trigger.
2 Focus is trapped Tab cycles within the dialog. The conversation behind it is inert, not merely covered.
3 Focus comes back On close, focus returns to the element that opened it. Delete confirmations return to the message, not the top of the page.
